Lockers and lost property

Lockers

Lockers are available to hire from Reception. Hiring a locker from Year 7 through to Year 12 (6 years) with a padlock is $120.00 (non-refundable). If you lose your padlock, a fee of $20.00 is charged for a replacement padlock. Only the official school padlocks may be used on your locker; non-school padlocks are not to be used and will be removed.

At the end of each year students are required to empty out their lockers for deep cleaning. They must take home their combination locks and keep them safe to be reused in the new school year.

Lockers for Year 7 students

A locker fee will be added to your Statement of Account as an "optional" charge. If the $120 fee is paid new Year 7 students will be allocated their locker number. Combination padlocks will be issued to students in Term 1, Week 2 2025. They must use the combination locks the school has provided.

Lockers for New Students

Students will be issued a combination lock. They must use the combination locks the school has provided. Any non-school lock used will be removed.

Lost Property

To assist in getting lost items returned to your child please ensure all uniform items and personal belongings are clearly labelled with your childs full name. This is especially important for items like jackets, jumpers and valuable items like laptops and wireless headphones. Any labelled items handed to the office will be returned to students.

If you have lost an item please check the lost property collection table at Reception. Valuables (phones, jewellery, laptops, ipads, instruments, watches, glasses, earbuds, keys, etc are kept inside the front office at Reception.

Details of the item/s lost can be left with Reception staff and if handed in, students will be notified to collect. If you suspect that your lost item has been stolen, report the matter to the Deputy Principal responsible for your year as soon as possible.

Money and Personal Property

Students are responsible for looking after their own money and valuables which they should carry with them at all times. Money should not be left unattended in school bags outside classrooms, in changing rooms or in the playground. All personal property should be clearly labelled.
