1. Fidelity

A reskin changes the visual design on purpose: the NSW school template is replaced by the One-AZ Figma kit. Pixel and SSIM scores therefore measure how far the new look is from the old site, not quality. Fidelity is reported on three axes:

  1. Visual similarity to the original — pixel and SSIM at the same viewport width (below).
  2. Content fidelity — every word and image carried; text is byte-equal on 52/52 pages.
  3. Design fidelity to Figma — tokens, type ramp, geometry and module anatomy gated against the Figma donor (§ 4).

1.1 How it was measured

  • Both sides captured by the branch's stardust/scripts/replica/stitch-shot.mjs --settle (full page, fonts and lazy images settled) at 1440 px for all 52 pages, and 768 / 390 px for the 3 archetypes.
  • Pixel similarity = 100 − pixel-compare.mjs differing-pixel %, threshold 100, compared over the overlap (the shorter page's height).
  • SSIM (0–100) on 4× downscaled greyscale: overlap, height-normalised (both resized to the same height — the fairer whole-page number) and above-the-fold (first 900 px).
  • Three runs are kept: before the header fix, after it, and the final run after the cards and footer fixes.

Pixel and SSIM are position-sensitive. Correct redesign work can lower them: the header fix removed a blank 529 px band that happened to match the original's white masthead. Run 3 is lower than run 2 (71.1 → 68.4) almost entirely because of 5 card-landing pages: /school-life 67.5 → 35.3, /learning-and-support 82.7 → 50.7, /about-us 64.5 → 34.4, /our-community 72.0 → 46.7, /about-us/why-choose-us 63.2 → 50.4. On those pages the original puts maroon image cards to the right of a sidebar nav (cards start at x≈536 px; on EDS at x≈130 px). Run 2 rendered the cards as a white bullet list, which happened to match the original's white sidebar and margins pixel for pixel. The fixed M2 cards are dark image cards like the source, but they start at the container edge (there is no sidebar), so dark pixels now overlap the original's white sidebar. The pages are visually closer (compare school-life) and their height error shrank (school-life +2,208 → −337 px), yet both metrics fall. The remaining gap is the missing sidebar (§ 1.3 Layout).
