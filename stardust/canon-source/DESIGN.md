## 1. Overview

**Creative North Star: "Clinical clarity with a warm brand accent."**

White pages with mist-grey hero bands, serif plum headings, and a single magenta action per block.

**Key Characteristics:**
- Lexia + Inter pairing
- plum headings, magenta actions
- 1180px column
- square media, 4px buttons

**Rule 1.** one primary button per block

**Rule 2.** headings in sentence case
